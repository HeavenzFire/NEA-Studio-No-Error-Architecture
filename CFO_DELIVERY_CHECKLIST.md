## 🎯 Presentation Flow

### Opening (2 minutes)
> "We've completed pre-validation testing of the Sovereign Labyrinth architecture. The results show epoch-scale resilience—15,855 simulated years in 24.8 seconds, zero Phase Lock violations. But these are just our results. What we're asking for today is authorization to prove this rigorously."

### Handover (1 minute)
*[Hand over CFO_VALIDATION_BRIEFING.md]*

> "This package contains everything you need: hypothesis, experimental design, success/failure criteria, independent verification framework, and known risks. The key question is on page 1."

### The Close (30 seconds + silence)
> "You don't have to believe me. You don't even have to agree with the hypothesis. You only have to decide whether the question is worth answering rigorously."
>
> *[Pause. Make eye contact. Wait.]*
>
> "If the answer is yes, sign here. If the answer is no, we document why and stop. Either way, we get truth."

### Response Handling

**If they ask technical questions:**
> "All technical details are in the appendices. The independent reviewer will verify every claim. What matters today is whether you authorize the inquiry."

**If they ask about the 110 errors:**
> "Known, bounded risks with documented remediation plans. The epoch-scale simulations—which stress-tested the core logic—had zero Phase Lock violations. The foundation is sound; we're hardening the edges."

**If they hesitate:**
> "That's valid. Would it help to see what a 'no' looks like? We document the reasoning, archive the analysis, and stop. No penalty for honest uncertainty."
