## Executive Summary

This document maps the **geopolitical adoption sequence** for the Autonomous Debt Dissolution Framework, identifying:
- **First Adopter Regions**: Healthcare-strained economies with maximum pain points
- **Diffusion Vectors**: Cross-border arbitrage, talent migration, competitive pressure

The model demonstrates that adoption follows a **"pain-first" trajectory**, starting in failing systems and cascading globally through engineered competitive advantages.

## 🎯 Regional Distress Index (RDI) Methodology

### Composite Scoring Formula

```
RDI = (H × 0.35) + (D × 0.30) + (I × 0.20) - (T × 0.15)

Where:
  H = Healthcare Burden Index (% of population with medical debt)
  D = Debt-to-GDP Ratio (household + government)
  I = Income Inequality (Gini coefficient normalized)
  T = Tech Infrastructure Readiness (broadband penetration, cloud adoption)
```

### Scoring Bands

| RDI Range | Adoption Probability | Timeline | Strategic Priority |
|-----------|---------------------|----------|-------------------|
| 75-100 | 95-99% | Months 1-6 | Critical - Deploy Immediately |
| 60-74 | 85-94% | Months 7-12 | High - Prepare Infrastructure |
| 45-59 | 70-84% | Months 13-18 | Medium - Monitor & Engage |
| 30-44 | 50-69% | Months 19-24 | Low - Educational Outreach |
