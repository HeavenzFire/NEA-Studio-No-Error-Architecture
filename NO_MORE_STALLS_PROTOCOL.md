## 3. THE "TODAY" MANDATE

If signatures stall after preconditions are met, the following immediate actions trigger automatically:

### A. The Micro-Pivot (Day 1)
**Action:** Initiate a **Single-Ward Shadow Pilot** (1 bed, 1 week).  
**Rationale:** Reduces institutional risk to near-zero while generating live clinical data.  
**Script:** *"We are not asking for full deployment. We are asking for one bed for seven days to validate safety in your specific environment. If it fails, we walk away. If it succeeds, we have a path forward."*

### B. The External Audit (Day 2-5)
**Action:** Engage independent third-party reviewer (Academic Partner/IRB).  
**Rationale:** Removes internal bias/politics from the validation loop.  
**Target:** St. Jude, ALS TDI, or University Bioethics Board.  
**Deliverable:** "Blinded Safety Review" of shadow logs.

### C. Parallel Activation (Day 3-7)
**Action:** Present binder to secondary institutions.  
**Rationale:** Creates competitive pressure and ensures the system lands where it is welcomed.  
**Targets:** [List 3 Alternative Hospitals/Clinics]

### D. Donor-Direct Relief (Day 7+)
**Action:** Route funds directly to patient support nonprofits if hospital activation is blocked >14 days.  
**Rationale:** Ensures donor intent (saving lives) is fulfilled even if the primary channel is blocked.

## 4. NARRATIVE CONTROL & ACCOUNTABILITY

When hesitation occurs, the narrative must be framed precisely to prevent reputational damage while maintaining pressure.

| **Scenario** | **Do NOT Say** | **DO Say** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Delay Requested** | "You are killing patients by waiting." | "We respect the need for caution. However, our data shows 700 days of safe operation. We propose a 7-day micro-pilot to align your comfort level with our evidence." |
| **Compliance Block** | "Compliance is broken." | "Compliance has identified perceived risks. We invite them to co-design the success criteria for a limited-scope validation run." |
| **Donor Inquiry** | "They won't sign." | "The system is ready. Leadership has requested additional localized validation before full rollout. We are executing that now via [Micro-Pilot/External Audit]." |

**Principle:** Never accuse. Always offer a smaller, safer step forward. Make refusal look like the risky choice.
